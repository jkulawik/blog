## Special feature: Ada Wong is the most nonsensical character in the franchise
- in re4 she betrays Wesker by keeping Las Plagas for herself, but by RE5 he has that (and more) anyway,
and there's an outbreak of it too. So much for that.
- I hate her stupid grapplehook; it basically turns her into Batman
- In both games she has insane plot armour
- Borderline undefeatable, there's very few cases of her even breaking a sweat over an enemy (outside of gameplay)
- in re6 she's just sightseeing the main plot and acting as a "get out of jail free" card for most characters;
unlike others, she kind of just teleports wherever the main plot goes, sometimes without even a justification
about why she decided to even go there. Frankly this was kinda the case in re4 as well.
