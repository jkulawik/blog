I have come to realize that these can be separate things; something I did not differentiate in the previous posts but will try to from now on.

"Story" is a narrative which has points to make or aims to evoke or show emotions. Plot is just the events portrayed in a narrative. For example, "The Last Of Us" has a plot about traversing a zombie post apocalypse, but it is a story about a broken man becoming an adoptive father after losing his biological daughter. In contrast, Resident Evil games all have some sort of a zombie plot, but almost none of them really tell any meaningful stories.

Witcher 2 does not have that kind of story really - nor does 1, for that matter. I don't treat this as something bad, as most games have plots rather than stories, and games with proper stories are still a rarity lauded as a big success.
