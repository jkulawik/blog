
# Bad

dialogue is too wordy, often completely unnecessarily - it's not the P1 rambling but rather just saying things in as many words as possible

avg dialogue in p2 was like 2 sentences, here it is 2 paragraphs

plague focus and elimination is annoying as fuck; plague districts are annoying as fuck in general

game is **very** long - it's runtime approaches the length of all 3 routes in p1 combined

things removed that were essential to pathologic:
- the fucking plague itself???? you cannot get sick
- survival mechanics are replaced by one "mental health" bar. This is perfectly fine on paper, but at times feels goofy.
Because of this, the destruction of water pipes does not affect you at all.
- theatre plays have been simplified and moved to spawn outside of the stillwater
- barter mechanics are barely in the game - you get a few generic trader girls in the town with an almost identical inventory each day,
a riot district trader and a few unique traders in stillwater.
There's barely any differentiation between their pricing and which items they accept, so trading feels like a traditional exchange system.
On top of that there is no money in this game, so you do not experience the price changes.

# Good

running around to rummage through trashbins, use water pipes and find playgrounds to trade with kids has been cleverly replaced by
making these objects affect bachelors mood

diagnosis mechanics, even if they get repetitive

lots of little fun clever things differentiating the bachelor from haruspex which tie into the themes of chess and time

decrees affect the town in fun little ways - some even affect gameplay (like the music interlude decree)

narrative framing with the inspector is cool - it is also cool to see the capital


# Ugly

models look much better than in the demo, but still have issues such as badly shaded eyelashes, eyebrows baked into the skin texture with their normals, clothing texture with lower resolution, sometimes goofy characters kit bashed from old models and new hair meshes,
there are even models with visible clipping. new models look amazing though

time travel is very interesting conceptually and makes for a fantastic story, although it being limited by amalgam can be a problem

rioting districts are neutered to the point of barely being a speed bump

due to time travel, the pressure to finish quests on a given day is just an annoyance rather than a real thing to make choices around

the events of the bachelor's story have been rewritten in a way that makes a lot of the story feel a bit pointless?
there is not much of a throughline in the plot, so day 3 and day 8 barely feel different at all.
Key aspects of the bachelor's story are just not there - namely him being misled and sent for goose chases a lot.
Generally you will experience very few moments which harkon back to P1's plot.

mind map is super messy, too many thought nodes are added around even linear quests

crafting is resource heavy and many recipes demand ingredients which are borderline unfindable - like valerian extract

constant quick travel means you don't have to learn the map and sleepwalk through the town a lot of the time.
batman vision doubles that problem

loot/trading economy/balance is a mess
